Battle of Delville Wood

The Battle of Delville Wood was a horrific battle early in the Somme campaign, with a casualty rate of 80%. Albert Marr and his pet baboon Jackie were present in the trenches for this battle, and were among the scant few to emerge without major injury. When the soldiers went over the trenches to attack, Jackie did as well, following their every move. Jackie had been trained to be a member of a human family, and had learned very quickly to be a member of a soldier family as well. Jackie was the first to alert when a sentry on night watch, using his sharp sight and hearing to detect advances from enemies, like the Boche. He was also kept around for entertainment when the trenches were not actively fighting.
