Medals, Ranks, and Recognition

During his service, Jackie the baboon had proved himself to be much more than a pet. He was an invaluable night guard, with senses sharper than any human, incredibly loyal as well as intelligent, and tough as any soldier. When Jackie and Marr were officially discharged on April 26, Jackie was sent off with one gold wound stripe and three blue chevrons for each year of service, as well as a military pension and a Civil Employment Form. After WWI, Marr and Jackie were initially sent to England, and Jackie immediately became a celebrity in the English newspapers. From September to February 14, 1919, the War Office and South African government lent Marr and Jackie to the Red Cross to collect money for sick and wounded soldiers. Once eventually arriving home in Pretoria, Jackie was the center of attention during the parade to officially welcome back the brigade, as well as the Peace Parade in Church Square, July 31, 1920. It was there that Jackie recieved the Pretoria Citizen's Service Medal and was also promoted to Corporal, a higher rank than any baboon had ever achieved.
