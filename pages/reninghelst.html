Retreat from Reninghelst

In April 1918, the South African Brigade was heavily shelled during withdrawal to Reninghelst, and Jackie attempted to build a wall during the retreat. He could not be convinced to move by anyone or anything, even as shells got closer. Even after the shrapnel from a shell wounded Jackie's arm and took off his leg as well, he still tried hobbling around to complete his wall and hide. Jackie's owner, Albert Marr, then picked him up and took him to the medics to save him as Jackie had saved his life in Agagia. The medics found it relatively easy to operate on Jackie, as Jackie took the chloroform like whiskey, and woke up just as easily as he had gone under. While he was under, the medics were able to easily cut off what remained of his leg with scissors, and then clean and dress the wound on his arm. After that, Jackie was sent to the Casualty Clearing Station by a passing ambulance, and that was the last of active duty for Jackie and Albert Marr.
