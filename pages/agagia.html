Battle of Agagia

The Battle of Agagia took place in Egypt, against the tribal Senussi rebels backed by the Ottoman Empire - which at the time, was Germany's ally. This was Jackie and Marr's first combat outside of the French trenches, and on the day of February 26, 1916, Albert Marr was shot in the shoulder. Jackie was extremely agitated, and licked Marr's shoulder until stretcher-bearers arrived and took them both away. Jackie never left Marr's side, and it was due to this incident and loyalty that Jackie became the favourite of the Regiment, as well as their comrade, rather than an unexpected pet.
