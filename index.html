Throughout his service, Jackie served steadily and impressed quite a few people, soldiers and civilians alike. This led to Jackie achieving the highest rank of any baboon, as well as a few other awards.

Life of Corporal Jackie

Jackie fought and lived alongside soldiers in the 3rd South African Infantry Regiment. He served in battles in French trenches against Germany, in Egypt against enemy tribes, and as the armies' most valuable night sentry. But who was Jackie before the Great War, and where did he go afterwards?
